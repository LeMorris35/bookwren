## First-week post ideas

1. **The challenge hook** (best one — pin AFTER launch settles): "First
   BookWren challenge is live: [X] pages by Sunday night. Join with code
   [CODE] at bookwren.app — leaderboard updates live. Winner gets bragging
   rights and a shoutout." *(Create the challenge in the app first, use its
   real invite code.)*
2. **The Wrapped tease**: post your own Wrapped card — "This is my year in
   books so far. Drop yours below 👇 bookwren.app/wrapped"
3. **The night mode reveal**: two screenshots side by side, wren vs raven —
   "Day reader or night reader? BookWren knows the difference. After 7 PM
   the library goes dark. 🌙"
4. **The exact-words flex**: "Most apps guess your pages. BookWren counts
   your words. 1,2 million words read this year? Prove it."
